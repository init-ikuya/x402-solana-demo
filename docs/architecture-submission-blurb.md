# Architecture submission blurb

This work-in-progress x402 demo on Solana connects an AI agent, a TypeScript gateway, and a facilitator; its exact payment and repository-specific Anchor-backed upto escrow flows are tested locally. The presentation focuses on a proposed Solana auth-capture binding in which a client holds funds, a merchant captures the final charge and releases the remainder, and the client can reclaim unused funds after a deadline. Merchant-funded refunds and the full auth-capture lifecycle remain design work, and live Devnet settlement has not yet been demonstrated.
